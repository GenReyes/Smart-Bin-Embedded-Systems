# (Bahga & Madisetti, 2014)

**APA 7 reference**

Bahga, A., & Madisetti, V. (2014). *Internet of things: A hands-on approach*.

Authors: Arshdeep Bahga and Vijay Madisetti. Self-published by the authors (the publisher is left out of the APA entry because it is the same as the author). Year 2014, confirmed on the WorldCat record for the title: https://search.worldcat.org/title/Internet-of-things-:-a-hands-on-approach/oclc/898528896

**What the paper uses it for**

Section 1.5, IoT Level. The book groups IoT systems into six deployment levels. Level 6 is described as having:

- multiple independent end nodes that do sensing and/or actuation and send their data to the cloud,
- data stored in the cloud, with a cloud-based application,
- an analytics component that processes the data and stores its results in the cloud database, shown by the cloud-based application,
- a centralized controller that knows the status of all end nodes and sends control commands to them.

The paper's explanation in 1.5 is a paraphrase of that description applied to Smart-Bin (two independent bins, Firebase and ThingSpeak as the cloud, the web dashboard and Android app as the centralized controller). It does not quote the book.
